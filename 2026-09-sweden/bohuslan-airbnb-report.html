Where to stay on the coast north of Gothenburg

I searched every Airbnb available for your three rural nights along the whole Bohuslän coast, from Marstrand up to the Norwegian border. 592 places were free for those dates. This report narrows them to one area and twelve properties, and shows the drive times that decide it.

The short answer

What the constraints in your brief actually point to.

Stay on the Skaftö peninsula, near Lysekil.

Your brief says the base has to be pretty to look at, and has to sit between the two groups of places you want to visit, with each within about an hour and ten minutes. Those two rules together only leave one part of the coast.

Go further north — Hunnebostrand, Smögen, Fjällbacka — and the northern day trips get easy but the Orust islands drop out of range. Go south to Orust or Flatön and the opposite happens. Skaftö and the small peninsulas around Lysekil are the only places that reach both, and they happen to have some of the prettiest villages on the coast: Grundsund and Fiskebäckskil.

My pick is the guest house in Grundsund at £234 for the three nights (£78 a night). It is a small building put up in 2021, it has its own patio, and it is in the best-looking village in the area. It has the strongest review scores of anything I found. If you would rather cut the driving down, the cottage at Skalhamn is 20 minutes closer to everything in the north and has a perfect five-star record, but the hamlet itself is plainer.

The rule in your brief is about an hour and ten minutes each way. Skaftö clears it in both directions. Hunnebostrand is 1 h 20 from the Orust ferry, and Flatön is 1 h 27 from Fjällbacka — both just outside. That is what removes them, not their looks.

One useful thing: the ferry across the Gullmarn fjord between Finnsbo and Skår is free, takes ten minutes, and runs roughly every twenty minutes during the day. It shortens the run east towards Uddevalla and Gothenburg. It is a Trafikverket road ferry, so you drive on and off with no booking.

What to know before you book

How solid these numbers are

  • Prices are live quotes from Airbnb for 28–31 August 2026, two guests, shown in GBP by Airbnb itself. They include cleaning and service fees. They can change, and several showed last-minute discounts that may disappear.
  • Ratings and review counts are as published on the listing on 23 August 2026.
  • Drive times are from Google Maps, free-flow, no traffic. Add a little for the Friday evening run out of the airport.
  • Amenities are taken from each listing's own amenity list. Where I say a garden is private or shared, that is the host's own wording.

Things I could not confirm

  • Whether cafés and restaurants in Grundsund and Fiskebäckskil are still open after 1 September. Several close for the season. Worth a phone call the week before.
  • The passenger ferry timetables to Käringön and Gullholmen from Tuvesvik drop to a reduced service in September. Check before you plan that day.
  • Boat trips and seal safaris mostly stop in mid-August. If you want the on-water day in your budget, book it before you go rather than turning up.
  • None of these hosts were contacted. Everything here comes from the listings.

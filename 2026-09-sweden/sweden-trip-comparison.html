Option A · The Coast

Bohuslän

Bare granite, white wooden villages, islands you reach on foot ferries. All three saved places are within 20 minutes of each other on Skaftö and Orust, so the choice of area is already made — only the house changes.

Option B · The Lakes

Dalsland, Vänern and Falköping

Lakes, canals, forest and red wooden farms. The seven saved places fall into three separate areas up to two hours apart, so here the area is part of the decision.

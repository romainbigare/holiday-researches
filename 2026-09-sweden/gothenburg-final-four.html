Four rooms in Gothenburg

Your shortlist, with the reviews actually read rather than the scores skimmed. Each of the four has one flaw that the star rating does not show you, and in two cases it is the sort of thing that decides the booking. Prices, breakfast, room sizes and walking distances are all verified below.

The short answer

Hotel Riverton if the budget stretches; Hotel Vanilla if it does not.

Riverton is the only one of the four that is both properly central and actually aimed at couples — a design hotel with air conditioning and a top-floor Skybar whose sunset gets named in review after review. It is also the most expensive, and its rooms are the weakest part of it.

Vanilla is 70 metres from Kungsportsplatsen — a one-minute walk, the most central address of anything you have looked at across all three rounds — and £50 a night cheaper with breakfast in. You pay for that in a hotel with no lift and a breakfast that recent guests say has gone downhill.

I would rule out Liseberg Grand Curiosa. It is comfortably the best-rated hotel here — 9.0 on Booking from 8,171 reviews, 4.5 on Tripadvisor — and none of that is aimed at you. It is a children's hotel: your 29 m² “Liseberg Room” contains a bunk bed and a single bed, there is an indoor slide and a carousel in the lobby, and guests describe the breakfast as “chaotic with LOTS of kids”. For a honeymoon it is the wrong building, however good it is at its actual job.

Hotel Vanilla — Superior Double, 20 m²

3★ · Kyrkogatan 38 · 8.3 Booking (2,241) · 4.1 TA (323)
Centre70 m · 1 min

A small, genuinely cosy hotel with a courtyard, 70 metres from Kungsportsplatsen — location 4.76, the best mark any of the four earns on any dimension. Breakfast included, and £100 cheaper than Riverton across the two nights.

“Friendly staff, unbeatable location and cozy courtyard contribute to all expectations met. The room was clean and comfortable.”4★

Two flaws

There is no lift. Two separate reviewers raise it, one describing “a large, circular staircase”. With suitcases off a road trip that is a real consideration. And the breakfast has been cut back — two independent 2026 reviews say so, and staff apparently confirmed it to one of them.

“There is no lift and also no help to carry the suitcases up, the rooms are not clean…”1★
“Breakfast was boring, scrambled eggs but no bacon, no sausages, some cold ham… Staff said owners have stopped serving bacon… They close the lobby at 8 pm… the rooms even though it says they are premium have no space.”2★ · Jul 2026

  • No lift · lobby closes 20:00 · street, seagull and bin-lorry noise reported
  • Value 3.94 and rooms 4.07 — the two lowest scores across all four hotels

Quality Hotel Waterfront — Standard Double, 27 m²

4★ · Adolf Edelsvärds Gata 10, Klippan · 8.3 Booking (1,709) · 4.1 TA (910)
Centre3.9 km · 47 min

A converted historic porter brewery on the Klippan quayside, industrial shell with contemporary fit-out. 27 m² and a super-king for £134.28 with breakfast is the best space-per-pound here, and the water views and breakfast are praised consistently.

“Our favorite hotel as it is beautifully located, has good price and the possibility of parking. In addition, we love the breakfast!”4★ · 1 Aug 2026

Two flaws

It is not where the name suggests. This is not the central Opera-side quay — it is out at Klippan, near the Denmark ferry, 3.9 km and a 47-minute walk from Kungsportsplatsen. Every trip into town is a tram ride. And housekeeping draws the worst complaints of the four, with cleanliness at 4.32.

“After arriving in our room, my wife discovered residue of stool in the toilet… a thick dust layer… Not washed in several years after my judgment.”2★ · 12 Aug 2026
“Poor standard and service… Bathroom and shower were nasty and otherwise poorly cleaned… missing towels…”1★ · 27 Jul 2026

  • Four of the ten most recent reviews are 2★ or worse; one reports being walked

Liseberg Grand Curiosa — Liseberg Room, 29 m²

4★ · Herman Lindholms Torg 1 · 9.0 Booking (8,171) · 4.5 TA (801) · “Mid-range, Family, City View”
Centre2.1 km · 25 min

On paper it wins everything: the highest scores in the shortlist, every subrating at 4.6 or above, the biggest room, breakfast included, opened 2023 so nothing is worn. It is a beautifully made hotel. It is also, unambiguously, built for children.

“The hotel is unique as it is actually centering children and play… They have a huge indoor slide next to the stairs… a café, a restaurant, a sweet shop, SEVERAL rooms with different themes ALL for children to play in.”5★ · 5 Aug 2026
“Very nice room where the children got their own small room with bunk bed… The only minus was that the breakfast was a bit chaotic with LOTS of kids and people. Hard to find a place!”5★ · 9 Aug 2026

The flaw

Your 29 m² is 29 m² because it sleeps five — the bunk bed and single bed are part of the room you would be booking. Add a carousel in the lobby, a 25-minute walk from town and no air conditioning, and the best-scoring hotel here is the worst fit for the trip you are actually taking.

  • Plus priority support, and the level is yours for life.

What I could not check

What we found

Two things matter most here, and neither one is the price.

Where you stay matters more than what you pay. You asked for a base with everything about an hour and ten minutes away. Very few places manage that. If you stay on Orust or Tjörn, Skärhamn and Pilane are under 50 minutes, but Smögen and Fjällbacka are over 100. If you stay at Kungshamn or Fjällbacka, it is the other way round. Smögen is 13 minutes, but Tjörn is 100 minutes or more.

Skaftö is the one place that works in both directions. From Fiskebäckskil or Grundsund, the closest trip is Lysekil at 35 minutes and the furthest is 95 minutes. Five of the seven trips are under 75 minutes. You also reach Skaftö by bridge, so there is no ferry to catch on the day you arrive or the day you leave. Both villages there are lovely. Click any pin on the map to see its own drive times.

We could not search Airbnb. There is no Airbnb connector available, and Airbnb blocks us from reading its pages directly, so nothing here comes from Airbnb. Instead we searched the whole houses, cottages and apartments on Booking.com, which is much of the same sort of place. Those are marked whole place in the list. Two are worth your time: Haere Mai has the best position of anything we found, and Skönaste stället is the best value at £101 a night.

The busy weekend shows in what is left, not just in the price. Lådfabriken, Salt & Sill and Stora Hotellet all have no rooms free on Booking.com for these three nights. Lådfabriken is the best-rated place we found, at 4.9 out of 5. It is worth calling all three directly before you book anything else.

How we did this

Airbnb

We could not search Airbnb. There is no Airbnb connector set up, and Airbnb returns an error when we try to read its search pages directly. The whole houses and apartments here all come from Booking.com. If you want Airbnb covered properly you would need to look yourself, or add an Airbnb connector.

Currency

All prices are in pounds. Booking.com gave us pounds directly. TripAdvisor gave us dollars, which we converted using your rates: £1 = 12.89 kronor and $1 = 9.53 kronor, so £1 = $1.35.

Distances

We measured every drive time on real roads from Landvetter airport, then checked them against published road distances. None of them are straight-line guesses. They assume clear roads.

The first and last day

If you leave the airport at 18:15, Skaftö is a 1 hour 56 minute drive. You would arrive around 20:10, just before sunset at 20:20. Going home, leave by 09:30 to reach the airport at 11:30. Both fit inside your two-hour limit, so you do not need the extra half hour.

Where we found nothing

Grundéns Hotell has no TripAdvisor page, and Hotel Strana has no rating yet because it is new. Neither one is badly rated. Where we could not find a price, we have said so rather than guessing.

Before you book

Call or email the week before to check. The three fully booked places may free up rooms. Small restaurants and boat trips on this coast often close after mid-August. Also worth knowing: the lobster trips Grebbestad is known for do not start until late September, which is too late for your dates.
